## Game Overview

Lords Mobile is an IGG strategy game built around building upgrades, research, troop training, hero and formation systems, guild play, and optional in-app purchases. For this offer, the important point is not general kingdom power. The important point is which systems move the exact CPAlead tasks: Castle, Academy, Player EXP, PvP attack proof, Mall purchases, and tracking support.

## Task-Moving Game Systems

### Castle
Castle progress moves through prerequisite buildings, basic resources, construction timers, speedups, and proof screens. Castle Level 5 should be treated as an early checkpoint, not a reason to spend. Screenshot the Castle info panel when the level is visible.

### Academy
The Academy is the research building. Academy level 1 is a simple early building task, but Academy level 25 is a different category. Academy 25 requires a broad prerequisite chain with Castle, key buildings, special buildings, and item gates such as Gold Hammers, War Tomes, Soul Crystals, and Steel Cuffs. That is why the Academy 25 task belongs in the high risk band even though its listed payout is attractive.

### Player EXP
Character level should be treated as Player EXP/profile level. Player EXP can come from monster hunting, equipment forging, admin quests, guild quests, troop engagements, hero stages, Colosseum, Turf Quests, and Player EXP items. Levels 15, 33, and 38 are checkpoints. Level 60 is the late wall because the EXP curve rises sharply near the top.

### Mall and purchases
The Mall sells packages and bundles for real money or Diamonds. For CPAlead, the pack name and timing matter more than the item contents. Keep the pack screen, receipt, reward screen, and CPAlead task row. The New Kingdom pack is a trap task if bought before day 8 and is negative gross spread before taxes/fees.

### Guild helps and quests
Guild systems matter because they support monster hunting, guild quests, and building help. An inactive guild makes Academy 25 and level 60 weaker. Guild play should accelerate the offer route, not pull you into expensive PvP or power padding.

## Random Reward Model

No task asks you to open a gacha or random chest directly. Randomness still matters because monster drops, quest rewards, event rewards, and some pack contents can vary. Guaranteed sources are the exact purchase action and visible pack contents. Random sources are monster or quest rewards and any pack item shown as chance-based. Screenshot reward previews before claiming or buying, and do not spend for random contents unless the guaranteed items visibly solve the bottleneck.
