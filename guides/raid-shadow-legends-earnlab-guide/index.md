## How Raid Works

Raid: Shadow Legends is an Android turn-based RPG from Plarium with campaign battles, champions, gear, dungeons, clans, arena, events, and in-app purchases. The EarnLab task route mostly uses four game ideas: campaign progression, account level, champion rank, and Sacred Shards.

Your first goal is not to build a perfect account. Your first goal is to make one starter champion strong enough to clear campaign stages, carry weaker food champions, and keep account progress moving. Campaign battles are also where the 20-battle task starts, where early champion XP comes from, and where you learn whether your energy use is efficient.

Account level is separate from champion level. Account level moves as you play and claim progress sources such as daily quests, missions, challenges, and regular campaign activity. Champion level and champion rank are handled through the Tavern rank route. A champion has a rank cap: Rank 1 caps at level 10, Rank 2 at level 20, Rank 3 at level 30, Rank 4 at level 40, Rank 5 at level 50, and Rank 6 at level 60. To increase rank, the champion must hit the rank cap and then consume same-rank champions or chickens.

Sacred Shards are not normal early-game drops you can assume will appear on demand. Sources discuss monthly quests, progression rewards, packs, and late-game routes, but the 6 and 8 Sacred Shard tasks should be treated as decision points. If you cannot clearly see how the next shard arrives, do not buy random packs hoping the offer will work out.

## Game Systems That Move Tasks

### Campaign Progression

Campaign progression is the backbone of the first week. It supports Fight 20 battles within 24 hours, starter champion leveling, food champion leveling, gear drops, and basic account activity. Use campaign progression before you get distracted by every menu. Your daily repeatable loop is simple: spend energy in campaign progression, claim daily quests, check missions and challenges, capture a task page screenshot, and reassess before spending.

### Tavern Rank Route

The Tavern rank route is the core of every 5-star and 6-star task. Level the base champion to the rank cap, build same-rank food, use XP Brews or Instant Upgrade when appropriate, then rank the champion. For a 5-star champion, you are moving a Rank 4 champion to Rank 5. For a 6-star champion, you are moving a Rank 5 champion to Rank 6. The bottleneck is not the button. The bottleneck is food champion count, energy, silver, and not feeding useful champions by mistake.

### Energy Management

Energy decides how many campaign runs you can do. XP Boosts help champion XP from Campaign clears, and Raid Card is a paid subscription-style offer that can add campaign XP, Silver, and Multi-battle attempts. Treat those as accelerators, not requirements. Never let an accelerator push you into a purchase before tracking proof.

### Gear, Silver, and Starter Champion

Gear and silver matter because a stronger starter champion clears campaign more reliably. Artifacts can come from campaign, dungeons, forge, market, arena and other modes. Early on, gear should support campaign farming, not drain all silver for tiny upgrades. If your starter cannot clear stages, your account level route, Tavern rank route, and food farming all slow down.

### Missions, Quests, Challenges, Arena, and Clans

Missions, quests, and challenges give direction and resources. Daily quests are especially important because they keep account progress and rewards moving. Arena and clans are not the main offer path, but they can support activity, rewards, and proof that the account is active. Join an active clan when available because clans can unlock Clan Boss, Clan Shop, Clan Quests, extra Multi-Battles, Campaign Silver bonuses, and activity rewards.

### Promo Codes

Use promo codes only when you verify them from current official channels, Plarium's code page, or trusted current community code pages. Plarium says codes can be entered in game or through its web page, and some new-player codes have first-account or time limits. Do not use an unverified link code that could interfere with the tracked EarnLab install path. This guide does not publish active codes because code status changes too quickly.

## Purchase Analysis

Purchase tasks can look profitable in points, but they are only profitable if the reward value is higher than the pack cost and the task credits. Use the reward points, your own cash value, and your confidence in support proof. A repeatable daily purchase can add up quickly. A Sacred Daily Pack may help shard tasks, but it also moves the offer from play-focused to spend-focused. The Beginner Progress Pack after Day 7 and Box of Gems after Day 30 are timing traps: buying too early risks the task not matching the row.

Before any purchase, ask three questions:

- Does this purchase help a task I can still complete?
- Do I have the task page screenshot and profile ID screenshot?
- Would I still be comfortable if support took time or denied the claim?

If any answer is no, wait.
