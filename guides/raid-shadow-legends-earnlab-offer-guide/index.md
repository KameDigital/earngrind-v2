## Game Systems That Actually Move Task Progress

### Campaign farming for account XP

Campaign clears are the repeatable loop that moves account level tasks while also leveling food champions. It overlaps Level 15, Level 30, Level 50, Level 60, Level 70, 5-star, and 6-star tasks.

### Tavern champion rank upgrades

The Tavern converts leveled food champions into higher star ranks. It is the core mechanic behind every 5-star and 6-star hero task.

### Energy refill timing and XP boosts

Energy gates campaign farming and XP boosts make the same energy more valuable. Energy shortages create most deadline misses after the early account levels.

### Portal Sacred Shard opening

Sacred or Yellow Shards must be opened in the Portal and are not interchangeable with cheaper shard types. The shard tasks require a specific random reward inventory item and proof before and after opening.

### Android purchase receipt flow

Paid tasks need the exact in-game purchase, Google Play receipt, and task-state evidence. Purchase tasks create the biggest mismatch between listed payout and cash outlay.

Ignore systems that do not help the task list. Arena ranking, perfect gear builds, faction projects, and champion collection detours can be fun, but they are not the shortest path to account level, Tavern rank, Sacred Shard proof, or purchase credit.

## Day-by-Day Route With Continue Reassess Stop Rules

Day 0: start from EarnLab, install on Android, finish Register and start playing the game, Complete the tutorial, and Fight 20 Battles within 24 Hours. Continue if the install path is clean and the first task states look normal. Reassess if nothing appears after a normal tracking wait. Stop before spend if install or tutorial never tracks.

Days 1 to 3: push campaign farming, daily quests, and first Tavern upgrades for Reach level 15 within 3 Days and Upgrade a hero to 5 stars within 3 days. Continue if campaign clears are reliable and the first 5-star proof is clean. Reassess if food champions or silver are already short. Stop if you would need a purchase just to rescue the early 5-star deadline.

Days 3 to 5: focus on Reach Level 30 within 4 days and Upgrade 3 heroes to 5 stars within 5 days. Continue if Level 30 and three 5-stars overlap naturally through campaign farming. Reassess if the account can level but the food pipeline is weak. Stop rank expansion if three 5-stars would consume your campaign carry or require unplanned pack buying.

Days 6 to 14: move only overlapping goals: Upgrade a hero to 6 stars within 10 days and Upgrade 5 heroes to 5 stars within 14 days. The priority order is starter carry, food inventory, silver, then proof. Continue if a 6-star food chain is already visible. Reassess if the first 6-star is not close by the midpoint. Stop before buying resources unless earlier paid and free tasks have credited.

Days 15 to 21: evaluate Upgrade 3 heroes to 6 stars within 21 days and Reach Level 50 within 21 days together. A strong account may keep going because campaign farming raises account XP while food levels. Most casual accounts should treat three 6-stars as a stop condition unless the food is already built.

Days 22 to 35: Reach Level 60 within 35 days and the after Day 30 purchase task are tracking-exposure goals. Continue only with a clean credit history and consistent daily account XP. Reassess if each daily loop produces too little level progress. Stop if Level 60 requires buying energy or packs just to chase listed payout.

Days 36 to 60: Reach Level 70 within 60 days is the largest listed payout and the least casual target. Continue only if you are already close, have no support gaps, and can finish without new speculative spend. For most users, this is where the offer should be closed rather than chased.

## Support-State Model

Provider: EarnLab. Support packet fields: EarnLab user/account identifier; offer name; task name; points; completion time; Raid player ID; screenshots; receipts when paid. Risk triggers: early install or tutorial does not credit; purchase made before task visibility; Day 7 or Day 30 timing missed; Sacred Shard tier opened without before screenshot.

Use these states: not_started, clicked_installed, in_progress, credited, pending, missing_credit, support_submitted, denied_or_unresolved. If a task is credited, move forward according to the milestone matrix. If a task is pending, keep playing only inside the current low-risk band. If a task is missing_credit, stop spend and assemble support proof before doing more paid or late tasks.

## Proof And Missing-Credit Workflow

First, document the task before completion: EarnLab page, exact task text, points, deadline, and current in-game state. Second, complete the task in the tracked Android session. Third, capture the result screen and EarnLab status. Fourth, wait through the provider's normal tracking window before opening a missing-credit case. Fifth, submit a clean support packet with the task name, points, completion time, player ID, and screenshots in order.

For purchases, include the Google Play receipt, in-game delivery, and task-state screen. For level tasks, include the account profile showing Level 15, Level 30, Level 50, Level 60, or Level 70. For rank tasks, include the Tavern or roster star count. For Sacred Shards, include the inventory before opening and the Portal result after opening.

## FAQ

### Is the Raid Shadow Legends EarnLab offer worth it?

It is worth testing through the early free tasks if you are eligible. It is worth grinding only if Level 30, early 5-star tasks, and tracking are all healthy. It is not worth treating the 372.72 rounded headline value as realistic for a normal user.

### Should I buy packs for the EarnLab tasks?

Only after early tracking credits and only when the exact purchase task is visible. The Silver 500k and Daily Gem Pack rows have small listed spreads before tax. The Sacred Daily Pack and Beginner Progress Pack are negative before tax unless they support another goal. The Day 30 Box of Gems has timing risk.

### Do Sacred Shard tasks require a specific champion?

No. The measurable task is opening Sacred or Yellow Shards. The champion result is random, and the guide treats it as irrelevant to payout strategy. The proof focus is inventory before opening, Portal result after opening, and EarnLab tier state.

### What is the difference between hero stars and champion level?

The offer says hero, while Raid often says champion. A 5-star or 6-star task is about rank shown through the Tavern and roster star count, not simply leveling a champion to a numeric level.

### When should I stop chasing Level 70?

Stop unless you are already close, earlier tasks credited cleanly, and the remaining daily account XP path does not require speculative spend. Level 70 within 60 days has the largest listed payout, but it is also a long-window tracking and time-risk task.

### What proof should I keep if credit is missing?

Keep the EarnLab task page, install path, player ID, completion screen, task state, timestamps, and receipts for paid tasks. Missing-credit support is much weaker if you cannot show the task before completion and the game state after completion.
