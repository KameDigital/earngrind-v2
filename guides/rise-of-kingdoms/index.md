## How the game works

[Rise of Kingdoms: Lost Crusade on Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.lilithgame.roc.gp) is a strategy game from LilithGames. For this offer, you mainly need to upgrade City Hall, research troops in the Academy, raise your account power, and fill the Daily Objectives points bar.

Google Play says the game has in-app purchases and random items. None of the tasks on this card asks you to win a random item. Do not buy random items hoping they will finish a task.

### City Hall: follow the next building need

[City Hall](https://riseofkingdoms.fandom.com/wiki/Buildings/City_Hall) controls how high you can upgrade other buildings. You cannot always tap City Hall and upgrade it right away. The game may ask for a Wall or another building first.

Use this simple loop:

1. Open City Hall.
2. Read the next building need shown on the screen.
3. Save the listed [resources](https://riseofkingdoms.fandom.com/wiki/Resources).
4. Upgrade the needed building.
5. Go back and start the City Hall upgrade.
6. Take a screenshot when the new City Hall level is done.

Do not upgrade every building just because you can. Extra work can use the same materials and build time you need for a paid City Hall goal.

### Academy: unlock one troop type at a time

The [Academy](https://riseofkingdoms.fandom.com/wiki/Buildings/Academy) is where you research troop upgrades. The source used for this guide shows T2 access at Academy 8, T3 at 16, T4 at 21, and T5 at 25. Reaching the Academy level only opens the path. You must also finish the right [research box](https://riseofkingdoms.fandom.com/wiki/Buildings/Academy/Unlocks).

Pick one troop type. Open its locked research box and follow the arrows back through every needed box. Finish that one path before you work on another troop type. This saves materials and helps you prove the exact troop level.

### Power: make useful upgrades count twice

[Power](https://riseofkingdoms.fandom.com/wiki/Power) is the number shown on your account. The offer has goals at 1 million, 1.5 million, and 2 million power. The sources do not show one best upgrade for every player.

Try to gain power from City Hall work, Academy research, and other steps you already need for a direct task. Check your power before and after each finished upgrade. Avoid upgrades that raise power but leave you short of materials for City Hall or troop research.

### Daily Objectives: fill the points bar

[Daily Objectives](https://riseofkingdoms.fandom.com/wiki/Quests/Daily_Objectives) are small jobs that give activity points. The captured source shows the final chest at 100 points, which matches the task name.

Open the current Daily Objectives screen. Pick jobs that also help City Hall or Academy progress. Watch the points bar, then screenshot the bar or final chest at 100. Use the jobs shown in your game that day because the list can reset.

## Task route

### City Hall tasks

**Reach City Hall Level 8 (Complete within 25 days).** Finish the tutorial. Open City Hall and read the next needed building. Upgrade only that building, then start City Hall. Take a screenshot after level 8 is finished. A build still running is not finished proof. Stop before spending if the offer does not look active.

**Reach City Hall Level 12 (Complete within 25 days).** Start from level 8. Open City Hall and check the next building need. Save the food, wood, stone, and build time for that step. Do not use the same materials on extra power upgrades. Screenshot City Hall when level 12 is fully done.

**Reach City Hall Level 14 (Complete within 25 days).** Keep using the same loop: check the next needed building, save the materials, finish that building, then upgrade City Hall. Use level 12 as your checkpoint. Save City Hall 14 and the task status. Check again if you would need to spend money you did not plan to spend.

**Reach City Hall Level 20 (Complete within 25 days).** Keep your main build path aimed at City Hall. Check the next needed building before you use a speedup. Materials and build timers are the main problems here. Do not trust an old online table more than the live screen in your game. Save City Hall 20, the last needed-building screen, and the offer status.

### Power tasks

**Reach Power 1 Million (Complete within 25 days).** Screenshot your starting power. Work on City Hall and Academy steps that also raise power. Check the power screen after each finished build or research box. Save the starting number, one finished direct goal, 1 million power, and the task status.

**Reach Power 1.5 Million (Complete within 25 days).** Start from your 1 million proof. Keep City Hall needs and one troop research path ahead of extra power work. Avoid a power upgrade that uses materials needed for the next paid goal. Save the 1.5 million power screen. Check again if your city and research work no longer helps a task.

**Reach Power 2 Million (Complete within 25 days).** Use the same plan. Do not buy a random power boost just to make the number rise. Move from 1.5 million to the next useful City Hall or Academy step, then to 2 million. Save the power screen, one City Hall or Academy progress screen, and the task card. Stop if the last push needs unplanned spending.

**Complete Game Tutorial (Complete within 7 days).** Open the game from the TyrAds path on the same Android device. Finish the tutorial shown in the game. **Unlock any Level III Troops (Complete within 25 days).** The source shows T3 access at Academy 16. Start from your saved T2 goal. Pick one T3 box, follow the arrows back through each needed box, and keep your research on that path. Save the finished T3 box and troop screen. Academy 16 by itself is not enough proof. Check City Hall 25, Academy 25, all needed level-25 buildings, economy research, military research, and one final troop box in your own game. Write down each locked step and the materials it needs. City Hall 20 or T4 does not mean T5 is close. Save the final research box and T5 troop screen. Stop if the path needs unplanned purchases or the offer tracking is unclear.

## FAQ

### Is the Rise of Kingdoms T5 task worth trying?

Treat it as high risk. The T5 source puts it behind City Hall 25, Academy 25, more level-25 buildings, economy and military research, and one final troop box. The sources gathered for this guide do not prove that a new account can finish this exact task by the listed deadline. Decide on the early tasks first, then check your live T5 path before you spend.

### What counts as unlocking Level IV troops?

The Academy source shows T4 access at Academy 21. That level only opens the path. You still need to finish one T4 research box. Save the finished box and the troop screen. Do not rely on an Academy level screenshot alone.

### Should I buy the Growth Fund for this offer?

Only if your task card is active, the exact first-time item matches, and you already want the item. The task names a $14.99 cost and lists a $14.87 reward, before tax. Save the task card, item screen, receipt, and screen shown after purchase.

### What should I save if a task does not credit?

Save the original task card, finished goal screen, Player ID, finish time, Android device and account details, and a dated receipt for a paid task. Open the TyrAds help option for the missing task and send that small, clear proof packet.
