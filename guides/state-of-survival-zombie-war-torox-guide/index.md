## Systems that matter

### Headquarters and building gates

The Headquarters is the core settlement building. Wiki data describes it as the main building and the cap for other building levels. That matters because the offer asks for Headquarters levels 16, 20, 23, and 28. Those are not single-button tasks. They require the HQ itself, the Hero Precinct, secondary building gates such as Garage or Range at certain levels, resources, prosperity, and construction time.

Your practical route is to keep HQ prerequisites visible. Do not upgrade random buildings just because they are cheap if they do not move HQ, Hero Precinct, troop production, research, or power. When a HQ upgrade requires another building, start that prerequisite before you run out of resources or speedups.

### Power growth

The offer uses several power labels: 10w, 40w, 200w, 10m, and 90m. Preserve those labels when you compare against the app because the task list is the source of truth. Power should be treated as total account growth, not one narrow stat. Buildings, troops, research, heroes, training, and upgrades can all matter.

The early power tasks are useful checks. If you are not crossing early power goals while also moving toward HQ 16 and HQ 20, the late tasks are probably not getting safer. Avoid dumping all resources into a power spike that leaves HQ prerequisites behind.

### Alliance support

Join an active alliance early. The wiki describes alliance timer help, alliance store access, crates, and alliance tech. None of that guarantees completion, but it can reduce timer pressure and add resource opportunities. A quiet alliance is much less useful for this offer than one where members actually help construction and research timers.
